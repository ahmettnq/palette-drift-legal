Privacy Policy — Palette Drift

Last updated: August 28, 2026
The short version: Palette Drift does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet access at all — everything it stores (your level progress, settings, and streak) stays only on your device.

Who we are

Palette Drift is developed by TNQ Nexus ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Palette Drift Android app (the "App").

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only, using Android's standard app storage (SharedPreferences). This information is never transmitted anywhere and is not accessible to us or to any third party:

  • Which levels you've completed, and your best result for each
  • Your current and best daily-challenge streak
  • Your sound and haptics preferences
  • Whether you've seen the onboarding tutorial

If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted along with it. You can also clear it at any time from the App's own Settings screen ("Reset" under Progress).

Permissions the App uses

  • Vibration — used only to provide haptic feedback when you play (for example, a light tap when a move is blocked). This permission does not let the App access any data.

The App does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or network access.

Third parties

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services in the App. We do not share information with anyone, because we do not collect any information to share.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country). Because the App has no accounts, no network access, and no data collection of any kind, there is no personal information to inadvertently gather from any user regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the App adds features that do involve data collection — we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above before that version is released.
